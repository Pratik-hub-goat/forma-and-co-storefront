THE IDEA BEHIND FORMA

01 / 03

Everyday life is made of little things.

The mug beside your notebook. The lamp that softens an evening. The objects you reach for without thinking. Small things shape the feeling of a day.

FORMA & CO. is a fictional concept store exploring the meeting point between considered design and everyday living. Our imagined collection brings together home, desk and lifestyle essentials through a clean, calm and contemporary point of view.

This project is a creative exploration of how a lifestyle brand can feel online: thoughtful products, clear information and an experience that leaves room for the objects to speak.
